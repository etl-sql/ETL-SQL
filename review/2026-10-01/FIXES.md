Behavior changes are recorded in [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](../../BREAKING_CHANGES.md) for v0.20.0. No dependencies were added.

The cookie regression reproduced a remaining leak after explicit header stripping: the shared handler sent a server cookie to another port. Disabling automatic cookies closes that path and also prevents cookie state from crossing connector instances. APIs requiring cookies must configure explicit Cookie headers.

Excel still uses synchronous ExcelDataReader APIs and scans the selected sheet to determine its populated extent before returning a batch. The fix removes retained workbook/worksheet DataSets; it does not claim constant time to the first batch or eliminate the reader's own shared-string metadata.
