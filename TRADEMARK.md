# ETL-SQL Trademark Policy

This document outlines the policy of the ETL-SQL project regarding the use of the **ETL-SQL** trademark.

The goal of this policy is to ensure that the ETL-SQL name and brand remain trusted and associated exclusively with official releases of the software, while still allowing the community to discuss, build around, and promote the project.

---

## 1. Our Trademark

The trademark covers:
* The name **ETL-SQL™**
* The name **ETLV-SQL™** (Extract-Transform-Load-Visualize-SQL)
* The official ETL-SQL and ETLV-SQL logos and branding assets

These are trademarks of **Charles Clemens**.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Required)

You may use the name "ETL-SQL" without prior written consent for the following purposes, provided your use is truthful, non-misleading, and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement:

### 2.1 Description and Promotion
* Discussing, reviewing, or writing articles about ETL-SQL (e.g., blog posts, tutorials, books, or social media).
* Linking to the official repository at [github.com/etl-sql/ETL-SQL](https://github.com/etl-sql/ETL-SQL).
* Promoting the project or presenting at user groups and conferences.

### 2.2 Compatibility and Extensions
* Stating compatibility with ETL-SQL. For example, you may write:
  * *"Database adapter for ETL-SQL"*
  * *"ETL-SQL compatible connection provider"*
  * *"An unofficial plugin designed for ETL-SQL"*
* **Rule:** The name "ETL-SQL" must not be the dominant part of your project name, and it must be clear that your extension is third-party.

---

## 3. Restricted Uses (Prior Written Permission Required)

You must obtain written permission from Charles Clemens before using the trademark for any of the following:

### 3.1 Company, Product, or Domain Names
* Using "ETL-SQL" in the name of your company, commercial product, or service (e.g., *"ETL-SQL Pro"*, *"ETL-SQL Enterprise"*, *"ETL-SQL Consulting LLC"*).
* Registering domain names, social media handles, or package namespaces that contain "ETL-SQL" (e.g., *"etl-sql-cloud.com"* or npm/NuGet organization scopes called *"etl-sql"*).

### 3.2 Modified Versions (Forks) and Hosting
* If you distribute a modified version of the software (a fork), you **cannot** distribute it under the name "ETL-SQL". You must rename the software and use a distinct brand.
* You may state that your software is *"based on the ETL-SQL engine"* or *"forked from ETL-SQL"*, but the primary name of your application must be different.
* Hosting a commercial SaaS version of the engine and branding it as an official or endorsed "ETL-SQL Cloud" service.

### 3.3 Endorsement and Affiliation
* Any use that implies that you, your company, or your product is endorsed, sponsored, or certified by Charles Clemens or the ETL-SQL project.

---

## 4. How to Ask for Permission

If you wish to use the ETL-SQL trademark in a way not explicitly permitted by this policy, please contact us at **etlsqlsoftware@gmail.com** to request permission. We are happy to discuss licensing and official partnerships.
