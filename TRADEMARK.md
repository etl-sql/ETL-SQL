# Octocolee & ETL-SQL Trademark Policy

This document outlines the policy regarding use of the **Octocolee** and **ETL-SQL** names and brands.

**Octocolee™** is the distinctive product name for the engine. **ETL-SQL** is the descriptive
engine/technical name commonly used to refer to the same software (it describes the product's
function — Extract, Transform, Load with SQL — and is therefore a weaker, descriptive mark).
"Octocolee, an ETL-SQL engine" is the canonical phrasing.

The goal of this policy is to keep the Octocolee name and brand trusted and associated exclusively
with official releases of the software, while still allowing the community to discuss, build around,
and promote the project.

---

## 1. Our Trademark

The policy covers:
* The distinctive product name **Octocolee™**
* The descriptive engine names **ETL-SQL™** and **ETLV-SQL™** (Extract-Transform-Load-Visualize-SQL)
* The official Octocolee / ETL-SQL logos and branding assets

These are trademarks of **Charles Clemens**.

> mascot or any cat/octopus-cat hybrid (the Octocat is a registered trademark of GitHub, Inc.), and
> must not imply affiliation with or endorsement by GitHub.

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Required)

You may use the names "Octocolee" or "ETL-SQL" without prior written consent for the following
purposes, provided your use is truthful, non-misleading, and does not imply sponsorship or
endorsement:

### 2.1 Description and Promotion
* Discussing, reviewing, or writing articles about Octocolee / ETL-SQL (e.g., blog posts, tutorials, books, or social media).
* Linking to the official repository at [github.com/etl-sql/ETL-SQL](https://github.com/etl-sql/ETL-SQL).
* Promoting the project or presenting at user groups and conferences.

### 2.2 Compatibility and Extensions
* Stating compatibility with Octocolee / ETL-SQL. For example, you may write:
  * *"Database adapter for Octocolee"*
  * *"ETL-SQL compatible connection provider"*
  * *"An unofficial plugin designed for Octocolee"*
* **Rule:** The names "Octocolee" or "ETL-SQL" must not be the dominant part of your project name, and it must be clear that your extension is third-party.

## 3. Restricted Uses (Prior Written Permission Required)

You must obtain written permission from Charles Clemens before using the trademarks for any of the following:

### 3.1 Company, Product, or Domain Names
* Using "Octocolee" (or "ETL-SQL") in the name of your company, commercial product, or service (e.g., *"Octocolee Pro"*, *"Octocolee Enterprise"*, *"Octocolee Consulting LLC"*).
* Registering domain names, social media handles, or package namespaces that contain "Octocolee" (e.g., *"octocolee-cloud.com"* or npm/NuGet organization scopes called *"octocolee"*).

### 3.2 Modified Versions (Forks) and Hosting
* If you distribute a modified version of the software (a fork), you **cannot** distribute it under the name "Octocolee". You must rename the software and use a distinct brand.
* You may state that your software is *"based on the Octocolee engine"* or *"forked from Octocolee / ETL-SQL"*, but the primary name of your application must be different.
* Hosting a commercial SaaS version of the engine and branding it as an official or endorsed "Octocolee Cloud" service.

### 3.3 Endorsement and Affiliation
* Any use that implies that you, your company, or your product is endorsed, sponsored, or certified by Charles Clemens or the Octocolee project.

If you wish to use the Octocolee or ETL-SQL trademark in a way not explicitly permitted by this policy, please contact us at **etlsqlsoftware@gmail.com** to request permission. We are happy to discuss trademark permission and official partnerships.

The software license and trademark policy are separate. Apache License 2.0 permits commercial use,
modification, and redistribution of the code, but it does not grant broad rights to the Octocolee or
ETL-SQL names or logos.
